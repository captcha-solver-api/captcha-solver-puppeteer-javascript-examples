# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Positive behavior includes showing empathy, respecting differing viewpoints,
giving constructive feedback, accepting responsibility, and focusing on what is
best for the community. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, trolling,
insults, sexualized attention, publishing private information, or other conduct
that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to support@captcha-solver.com. Project maintainers
will review reports promptly, respect the reporter's privacy, and take fair and
appropriate corrective action. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments,
commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.
